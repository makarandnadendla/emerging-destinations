Causal DAG — origin development → within-Japan remoteness

Estimand: the effect of an inbound traveler's origin-country development (HDI; with GDP pc PPP, LPI, UHC, WGI as alternative operationalizations) on how off-the-beaten-path they go inside Japan (photo-weighted mean cell remoteness, remoteness = inverse OSM POI density). Identification strategy this DAG implies

  • Adjustment set = origin region + trip_year. These are the only true back-door confounders; they close HDI ← REGION → Y and HDI ← YEAR → Y.
  • Region does double duty — confounder and H3 effect-modifier. Estimate within-region HDI slopes (CATE by region) rather than one pooled ATE: that blocks the back-door and produces the heterogeneity headline.
  • Mediators stay open (budget, independent travel, trip length, self-drive). We want the total effect, so we do not condition on them. Corollary: GDPpc / LPI / UHC / WGI are alternative treatments, run separately — never co-adjusted with HDI.
  • Selection collider cannot be closed. The Flickr sample is fixed, so SAMPLE is conditioned by construction, opening HDI → Flickr → [SAMPLE] ← REGION → Y. Adjusting region blocks its final leg; residual selection is probed by the hour-of-day negative control, an E-value, and the stated-vs-modal home-resolution agreement check.
  • Design strength: the remoteness yardstick (OSM POI density) is exogenous to tourist behavior, and its error (rural under-mapping) is independent of origin HDI → non-differential → attenuation toward null, not a spurious effect.

Note: the outcome is photo-count weighted; because photo count is a descendant of trip length (a mediator) and Flickr engagement, an unweighted per-cell outcome is run as a sensitivity spec.
